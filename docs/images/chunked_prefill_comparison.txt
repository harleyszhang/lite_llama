
=== WITHOUT Chunked Prefill (v0.6) ===

Step 1: [PREFILL ████████████████████████████████] 2000 tokens (BLOCKING)
         Decode requests A,B,C,D: ⏸ STALLED

Step 2: [DECODE A] [DECODE B] [DECODE C] [DECODE D]
         (finally running after full prefill)

⚠ Decode latency spike: ~200ms during long prefill


=== WITH Chunked Prefill (v0.7, chunk=512) ===

Step 1: [PREFILL chunk 1/4 ████    ] + [DECODE A,B,C,D ████]
Step 2: [PREFILL chunk 2/4 ████    ] + [DECODE A,B,C,D ████]
Step 3: [PREFILL chunk 3/4 ████    ] + [DECODE A,B,C,D ████]
Step 4: [PREFILL chunk 4/4 ████    ] + [DECODE A,B,C,D ████]

✓ Decode runs EVERY step — no latency spike!
✓ 2000-token prefill split into 4×512 chunks


=== Performance Comparison ===

| Metric              | No Chunk | Chunk 512 | Improvement |
|---------------------|----------|-----------|-------------|
| Decode steps        | 0 (blocked) | 4 (every step) | ∞        |
| Max decode latency  | ~200ms   | ~4ms      | 50x better  |
| Prefill throughput  | Same     | Same      | No loss     |
| Time-to-first-token | Same     | Same      | No loss     |

Conclusion: Chunked prefill eliminates head-of-line blocking
            with ZERO throughput cost.
