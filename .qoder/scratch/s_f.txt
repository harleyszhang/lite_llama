### examples/benchmark.py | total=279 | doc=17L/794c | usage=False
DOC| Latency/throughput benchmark: lite_llama vs HuggingFace transformers.
DOC| 
DOC| Reports the metrics used by vLLM / SGLang serving benchmarks, per engine:
DOC| 
DOC| * **TTFT** (time to first token, s) — prefill latency, measured as the wall-clock
DOC|   time of a ``max_new_tokens=1`` generation over the batch (prefill + 1 token).
DOC| * **TPOT** (time per output token, ms) — steady-state decode latency, defined
DOC|   exactly as vLLM does: ``(latency - ttft) / (output_len - 1)``.
DOC| * **TGS** (token generation speed, tokens/s) — aggregate output throughput,
DOC|   ``total_output_tokens / latency``.
DOC| 
DOC| Results (and the full config) are printed and saved to ``--log-dir`` as JSON.
DOC| 
DOC| Run from the repository root:
DOC|     python examples/benchmark.py --model my_weight/Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct         --batch-size 8 --gen-len 128 --iters 2
